    Split housings, milled on multi-axis centers.

FAQ

Technical questions, answered.

Is Witco ITAR registered and cleared for defense work?

Yes. Witco is ITAR registered (CAGE 3FNW6), a government prime contractor, and a member of the Michigan PTAC and DC3. It has supplied the U.S. military for years as a second- and third-tier supplier: gun mounts and gun parts, vision system components, wheeled vehicle and tank components, and aircraft fuel system components.

Which exotic alloys can you machine?

Titanium, Inconel, Monel, Hastelloy, A286, Greek Ascoloy, 17-4PH and other precipitation-hardening stainless, tungsten and magnesium — plus platinum, palladium and iridium for medical work. Stock forms include bar, plate, tubing, forgings, extrusions, sawed blanks and investment, sand and die castings.

What does a Zeiss Prismo CMM report include?

Size, form and position from a single measuring run with the VAST probe, at sub-micron resolution. For gears and shafts up to 48", the gear package charts straightness, roundness, concentricity, lead error, tooth major/minor, runout, backlash and involutes. The CMM also supports reverse engineering: it reads dimensions and the data is used to build the machining program.

Can you plug into our EDI and Just-in-Time supply chain?

Yes. Witco exchanges 850/875 purchase orders, 855 acknowledgements, 856 advance ship notices, 810/880 invoices, 860 change requests, 997 functional acknowledgements and more. JIT programs include dock-to-stock delivery on daily, weekly or monthly intervals, bar-code labeling and custom reusable containers. Blanket orders bundle scheduled releases under one PO.

What order quantities do you take?

Typical orders run from 1 to 5,000 pieces — from a single prototype to recurring production. Repeat demand is best handled with a blanket order for volume pricing and steady scheduling.

How are our drawings and data protected?

Witco holds written confidentiality agreements with every supplier that may see project information, offers non-compete and confidentiality agreements to customers, never sells, rents or leases RFQ data, and deletes your files on request.

Do you handle finishing, heat treat and assembly?

Yes — through in-house operations and a network of NADCAP-certified suppliers for finishing, heat treat and finish grinding. Assembly ranges from insert installation to complex multi-level BOMs, using lean cells: one-piece flow, two-bin systems, mistake-proofing and visual work instructions.
